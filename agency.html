About Sofia

Producer and advertising student at Boston University working across production, campaign thinking, and social-first storytelling. I’m interested in ideas that are clear enough to travel, visual enough to remember, and practical enough to produce well.

My production background shapes how I think creatively. I don’t just ask whether an idea is interesting — I think about how it will be shot, edited, delivered, and experienced across platforms. That makes me useful in the space between concept and execution.
