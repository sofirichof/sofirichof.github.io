## AP Drawing portfolio, reviewed September 28, 2026

Source: `Sofia_Gonzalez_Irigoyen_AP_Drawing_Portfolio.pdf`, the 2022 submission found in Sofía’s iCloud Documents / Previous Academia / Documents misc. The original was read without modification. An unchanged reference copy is saved separately from this website; the full school submission is not included in the website package.

The source is one long PDF page. Artwork images were visually matched to their entries, not matched on text alone:

- **Guitar:** Sustained Investigation Image 15 and Selected Works Work 5. Gouache, wood panel, glazing medium and gesso; 24 × 18 inches. A stringless guitar represents a love song with no purpose following a breakup. The process combines abstract pointillism, motifs, light-source study, color matching and concept revision. Written Evidence Response 2 identifies this final piece as bringing together the skills developed throughout the year.
- **Hand / sphere:** Sustained Investigation Images 13–14 and Selected Works Work 3. Gouache, acrylic paint, wood panel, glazing medium and gesso; 9 × 12 inches. Reflection and retrospection on time and memory are made literal. Research included Escher and surrealism, a reference photograph, color matching and skin tones, and a recurring clock motif. Image 14 documents pastel studies, sketches and revisions.
- **Shared investigation:** Written Evidence Responses 1–2 describe the malleability of memory, the investigation’s turn toward personal experience, and abstract pointillism as a way to represent memories that are clear but imperfect.

The website uses concise, first-person adaptations of these notes in English and Spanish, labeled as adapted from the 2022 AP Drawing portfolio. They are not verbatim quotations or Instagram captions. Dimensions retain the source’s numeric order without assigning height/width: the sphere’s labeled orientation in the PDF does not match the pictured orientation. The existing artwork images, wall placement, and mirror portrait were not replaced.
